# AIPL-05 — Voice Studio

## Product

Convert a script into professional narration, timed subtitles, optional approved music, and a ready-to-use audio or video export.

- Primary track: AI Engineering
- Supporting tracks: Backend, Frontend, UI/UX
- Target users: educators, creators, churches, marketers, and course producers
- Status: Discovery

## MVP workflow

Script → voice/language selection → synthesis → pronunciation review → timeline preview → subtitle generation → WAV/MP3/MP4 export.

## Technical shape

Use a replaceable TTS provider/model, pronunciation lexicon, chunking, loudness normalization, job queues, preview caching, and explicit voice licensing metadata. Do not begin with voice cloning.

## Delivery phases

1. Curated licensed voices, script editor, audio preview, and download.
2. Pronunciation controls, subtitles, and simple visual video export.
3. Multilingual evaluation, templates, and measured fair-use controls.

## Success and safety

Measure intelligibility, pronunciation corrections, generation latency, export success, and cost per finished minute. Require voice rights and disclosure; block impersonation, fraud, and unauthorized cloning.

## Portfolio evidence

Voice/license register, quality evaluation, accessible editor, audio samples, cost model, and misuse-prevention design.
