# 0xVerse AI Product Lab

The AI Product Lab is a portfolio of product projects based on a shared principle: use increasingly affordable open-source AI capabilities to deliver a simple, valuable workflow for a specific customer. Students are building products and operating systems—not foundation models.

## Operating model

1. Select one project only after a tutor approves its problem and target user.
2. Open Issues from the project phases; assign students by track.
3. Build a narrow vertical slice before adding channels, languages, or formats.
4. Evaluate quality, cost, latency, privacy, and usability with a documented test set.
5. Submit work through reviewed branches and pull requests.
6. Mark work portfolio-ready only after safety, documentation, attribution, and reproducibility checks pass.

Every project follows [shared delivery standards](SHARED_STANDARDS.md) and the [track ownership model](TRACK_OWNERSHIP.md). Do not promise “unlimited” usage until real infrastructure costs, rate limits, abuse controls, and fair-use policies have been measured.
