# AIPL-04 — African Media Intelligence

## Product

Turn English, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, and mixed-language audio into editable transcripts, translations, summaries, subtitles, and documents.

- Primary track: AI Engineering
- Supporting tracks: Data Analytics, Backend, Frontend
- Target users: journalists, churches, schools, researchers, podcasts, and media houses
- Status: Discovery

## MVP workflow

Upload/record → language and quality assessment → transcription → speaker/timestamp editing → optional translation → summary/subtitles → DOCX/SRT/VTT export.

## Technical shape

Build language-specific evaluation sets with consented/licensed audio, a replaceable ASR layer, segmentation, glossary support, editable transcripts, and per-language quality reporting. Mixed-language audio must be evaluated separately.

## Delivery phases

1. English/Pidgin transcription editor and subtitle export.
2. One additional language with native-speaker evaluation and glossary support.
3. Translation, summaries, speaker workflows, and expanded languages.

## Success and safety

Measure word error rate by language/accent, subtitle timing, editing time, and perceived quality from qualified reviewers. Obtain consent, protect sensitive recordings, expose uncertainty, and never market unsupported language quality.

## Portfolio evidence

Dataset card, per-language evaluation, transcript editor, subtitle samples, consent/privacy design, and an honest limitations report.
