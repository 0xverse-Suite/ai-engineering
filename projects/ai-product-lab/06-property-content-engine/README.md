# AIPL-06 — Property Content Engine

## Product

Turn approved property photos and facts into enhanced images, descriptions, social posts, WhatsApp listings, flyers, short videos, and an SEO-ready listing page.

- Primary track: AI Engineering
- Supporting tracks: Frontend, Graphics Design, Data Analytics
- Target users: real-estate agents and small property firms
- Status: Discovery

## MVP workflow

Upload photos/facts → validate required fields → enhance approved images → generate factual copy variants → compose branded preview → human fact check → export.

## Technical shape

Separate authoritative facts from generated language, use template-based graphics, preserve originals, record transformations, and prevent unsupported claims. Begin with one listing format and one social channel.

## Delivery phases

1. Fact form, description, caption, and static flyer preview.
2. Image enhancement, brand templates, and multi-channel exports.
3. Short video and analytics-informed content iteration.

## Success and safety

Measure correction rate, time saved per listing, factual error rate, export success, and engagement experiments. Never fabricate amenities, prices, locations, or legal status; remove EXIF/location data where needed; require image rights.

## Portfolio evidence

Before/after samples, factuality test, template system, workflow demo, brand guide, and privacy/licensing checklist.
