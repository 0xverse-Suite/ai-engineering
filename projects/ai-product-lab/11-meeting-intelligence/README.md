# AIPL-11 — Meeting Intelligence

## Product

Turn an authorized meeting recording into an editable transcript, concise minutes, decisions, owners, due dates, and follow-up actions.

- Primary track: AI Engineering
- Supporting tracks: Backend, Frontend, Fullstack
- Target users: teams, schools, nonprofits, and small businesses
- Status: Discovery

## MVP workflow

Consent/record or upload → transcription → speaker/timestamp review → decision/action extraction → participant confirmation → minutes export.

## Technical shape

Use a replaceable ASR layer, speaker labels without identity claims, structured action schemas, transcript citations, retention controls, and optional calendar/task export only after confirmation.

## Delivery phases

1. Upload, transcript editor, summary, and minutes export.
2. Evidence-linked actions, owners/dates, and confirmation workflow.
3. Approved meeting/task integrations and team analytics.

## Success and safety

Measure transcription accuracy, action precision/recall, editing time, and confirmed-minutes rate. Require participant consent, avoid covert recording, protect sensitive discussions, and never create external tasks without confirmation.

## Portfolio evidence

Consent flow, action-extraction benchmark, transcript/minutes UI, integration design, retention policy, and a synthetic meeting demo.
