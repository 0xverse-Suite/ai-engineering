# AIPL-08 — Lecture Learning Assistant

## Product

Turn a lecture recording into an editable transcript, clean notes, summary, flashcards, quiz, possible study questions, and an exportable study pack.

- Primary track: AI Engineering
- Supporting tracks: Frontend, Mobile, UI/UX
- Target users: students, tutors, schools, and training providers
- Status: Pilot candidate

## MVP workflow

Upload consented lecture → transcription → transcript correction → topic segmentation → notes/summary → cited flashcards/quiz → learner review → PDF/CSV export.

## Technical shape

Ground every generated item in timestamped transcript evidence, use replaceable ASR/LLM layers, separate instructor material from learner uploads, and provide an accessible study workspace.

## Delivery phases

1. Transcript editor, notes, summary, and PDF export.
2. Evidence-linked flashcards and quizzes with quality evaluation.
3. Mobile capture, spaced review, and tutor-approved course integration.

## Success and safety

Measure transcript accuracy, factual grounding, learner correction rate, quiz validity, completion time, and study usefulness. Obtain recording consent; never claim predicted exam questions are authoritative; protect educational records.

## Portfolio evidence

Grounding evaluation, learner usability study, accessible interface, study-pack examples, consent design, and cost/latency report.
