# AIPL-07 — Commerce Catalogue Factory

## Product

Turn a product photo, verified product facts, and price into cleaned images, catalogue descriptions, SEO copy, marketplace listings, and social/WhatsApp content.

- Primary track: AI Engineering
- Supporting tracks: Frontend, Graphics Design, Data Analytics
- Target users: online sellers and small retail businesses
- Status: Discovery

## MVP workflow

Upload image/facts → background cleanup → fact validation → copy generation → channel-specific preview → merchant correction/approval → export.

## Technical shape

Use background-removal/image-enhancement models, schema-bound product facts, reusable copy templates, channel constraints, a brand profile, and structured exports. Generated copy must never override merchant-provided facts.

## Delivery phases

1. Single image, description, clean background, and one catalogue export.
2. Brand voice, batch products, and social/marketplace variants.
3. Catalogue analytics and approved commerce integrations.

## Success and safety

Measure merchant correction rate, image acceptance, completion time, batch failures, and cost per product. Do not alter product identity, invent benefits, copy competitors, or process prohibited goods.

## Portfolio evidence

Product schema, image/copy evaluation, catalogue demo, template library, batch-processing architecture, and responsible-commerce policy.
