# AIPL-10 — Content Repurposing Suite

## Product

Turn one approved video, podcast, webinar, or article into a coherent month of channel-specific posts, hooks, captions, newsletter copy, blog content, and short-form briefs.

- Primary track: AI Engineering
- Supporting tracks: Frontend, Graphics Design, UI/UX
- Target users: creators, educators, marketing teams, and small businesses
- Status: Discovery

## MVP workflow

Import source → extract transcript/text → identify themes and claims → choose brand/channel plan → generate linked content variants → editorial review → calendar/export.

## Technical shape

Store a source-of-truth content graph, preserve claim provenance, use channel constraints and brand profiles, detect repetition, and provide an editor rather than automatic publishing.

## Delivery phases

1. One source to LinkedIn, Instagram, and newsletter drafts.
2. Brand voice, content calendar, visual briefs, and quality feedback.
3. Team review, analytics experiments, and approved publishing integrations.

## Success and safety

Measure editorial acceptance, factual consistency, duplication, time saved, and engagement experiments. Require content rights, prevent fabricated quotes/claims, label synthetic assets, and never auto-publish without confirmation.

## Portfolio evidence

Content graph, provenance demo, editorial interface, brand system, output evaluation rubric, and a licensed-source campaign case study.
